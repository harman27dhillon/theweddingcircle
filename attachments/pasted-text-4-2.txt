Fix Album Heading Contrast and Make Start Live Stream Button Prominent

1. Album page — remove the extra text

In the Album section, the current page displays:

- “The album”
- “Everything shared in the circle, kept with the function it belonged to. It stays after the wedding.”
- “The rest of the day”

These headings and descriptions are almost invisible because their light-colored text clashes with the selected theme's background.

Required changes:

- Remove the introductory heading “The album”.
- Remove the descriptive paragraph below it completely.
- Remove the subheading “The rest of the day”.
- Replace all of them with just one clean heading: “Album”.
- Display “Album” in a dark, readable color, preferably a deep brown or near-black that contrasts clearly with the current theme background.
- Position the heading neatly at the top of the Album content, with balanced spacing before the photo grid.
- Keep the existing photo count, photo grid, image previews, Save buttons, album data, and all existing functionality unchanged.
- Ensure the heading remains readable across every theme, including light, dark, and custom themes. Use theme-aware contrast or an appropriate dark heading color for light backgrounds and a suitable contrasting color for dark backgrounds.

Do not leave empty space where the removed text used to be. Make the Album page compact and visually balanced.

2. Live camera — make the Start Live Stream button stand out

In the camera/live preview screen, the bottom controls currently show five circular buttons: video camera, microphone, camera switch, live broadcast, and close (X).

The live broadcast button (the fourth button, immediately to the left of X) is not prominent enough. It looks too similar to the other secondary controls, even though it starts a live stream.

Required changes:

- Keep all five controls in their current order and position.
- Make the fourth button visually prominent as the primary action.
- Give it a larger visual presence through a clearly differentiated background, brighter broadcast icon, and stronger contrast against the camera preview.
- Use a premium wedding-appropriate treatment: a rich burgundy or deep red background with a champagne-gold border or subtle glow, consistent with the existing Wedding Circle design.
- Keep the X button visually distinct as the close action, not another primary action.
- Add an accessible label or tooltip such as “Start live stream”.
- If the live stream is already active, show a clearly different active state and label, such as “Live” or “End live”, according to the existing functionality.
- Preserve the current camera preview, full-screen behavior, microphone, video controls, camera switching, permissions, and live-stream logic. Do not accidentally trigger the stream merely by opening the camera preview.
