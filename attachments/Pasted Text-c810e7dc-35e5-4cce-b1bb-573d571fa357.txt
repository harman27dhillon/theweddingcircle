TASK: ADD ELEGANT GOLDEN SPARKLES AND SPECIAL HIGHLIGHTING TO BRIDE AND GROOM CHAT MESSAGE BUBBLES.

IMPORTANT:
Modify only the existing Chat UI and its message styling. Preserve the recently repaired message alignment, message identity logic, message history, and all existing chat functionality.

Do not redesign the Chat screen or modify unrelated screens.

1. IDENTIFY THE BRIDE AND GROOM

Inspect the existing wedding data model to determine how the bride and groom are identified.

Use the wedding's actual bride and groom identities and stable user IDs wherever available.

Do not identify them through hard-coded display names.

Only messages belonging to the bride or groom should receive the special wedding sparkle treatment. Other participants' messages must retain their normal styling.

The special treatment must follow the bride and groom's messages regardless of who is viewing the conversation or which side of the screen the messages appear on.

2. CONTINUOUS GOLDEN SPARKLE ANIMATION

Add small, elegant champagne-gold sparkles that continuously revolve around the OUTER BORDER of the bride's and groom's message bubbles.

Animation requirements:
- Small, refined gold sparkles rather than large stars.
- Sparkles travel continuously around the message bubble's perimeter.
- Use a smooth, seamless looping animation.
- The animation must follow the actual bubble shape, including rounded corners.
- Support bubbles of different widths and heights, including multiline messages.
- Keep sparkles close to the outer border without covering the message text.
- Use subtle gold glimmers and occasional tiny twinkles.
- Keep the effect elegant and premium, not flashy or distracting.
- The animation should continue while the chat is open, including when new messages arrive.
- Do not restart every sparkle whenever the user types or a new message is received.

Do not simply rotate a static star image or rotate the entire message bubble. The sparkles themselves should travel around the perimeter.

3. SPECIAL MESSAGE BUBBLE HIGHLIGHTING

Give the bride's and groom's message bubbles a slightly enhanced appearance:
- A subtle luminous border.
- A delicate champagne-gold edge highlight.
- A very soft outer glow.
- Refined depth that complements the existing rounded bubble shape.
- A restrained highlight that makes their messages stand out without harming readability.

The effect must remain subtle enough that the conversation is easy to read.

Do not make the entire chat screen glow.

Do not enlarge the bride's or groom's bubbles or change their alignment, padding, shape, or position just to create the effect.

- Ensure the animation works with different message lengths and on phone-sized screens.
