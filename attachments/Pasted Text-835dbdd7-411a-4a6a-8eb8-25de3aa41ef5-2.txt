PHASE 3 — Build a Bride-Focused Discover Section

Transform the existing Discover button/section into a dedicated destination for Indian bridal fashion, jewellery, makeup, and accessories.

Discover should feel like a premium, visually appealing product-discovery experience within The Wedding Circle. It must not turn the main wedding landing page into a shopping marketplace.

3.1 Discover navigation and categories

Remove the unrelated existing buttons and categories inside Discover and replace them with these relevant categories:

- All — every eligible product discovered by all configured bots.
- Dresses — bridal lehengas, sarees, wedding outfits, reception dresses, and related bridal clothing.
- Jewellery — bridal necklaces, earrings, bangles, maang tikka, nath, kaleere, and jewellery sets.
- Makeup — bridal makeup products, palettes, lip products, foundation, and related beauty essentials.
- Footwear — bridal heels, juttis, sandals, and wedding footwear.
- Accessories — clutches, potli bags, veils, dupattas, hair accessories, and other relevant bridal accessories.

Add further bridal-specific categories only when they are useful. Avoid unrelated shopping categories, duplicate category buttons, or unnecessary navigation.

The All category should be the default view when Discover opens.

3.2 Product grid

Display products in a compact, responsive grid.

- Each product tile must have a 1:1 square aspect ratio for its main image.
- On mobile, use a balanced multi-column grid that keeps products visible and easy to browse.
- Use a suitable responsive column count on larger screens.
- Use consistent image dimensions, spacing, corner radius, and theme-aware backgrounds.
- Preserve image quality while using appropriately sized thumbnails for fast loading.
- Prevent stretched, distorted, or incorrectly cropped product images.
- Where appropriate, show the product name, current price when verified, source retailer, and a small popularity/trend indicator backed by real data.
- Clearly distinguish sponsored placements, if any, from organic results.
- Provide loading, empty, unavailable, and error states.
- Support pagination or incremental loading rather than loading an unlimited product catalogue at once.

The All category must combine eligible results from all enabled bots without showing duplicate copies of the same product. Keep product cards consistent across every category.

3.3 Product detail and original-source link

When a user opens a product tile, display an in-app product detail page or overlay that includes the available verified information:

- Product image or gallery.
- Product name and description.
- Retailer or original source name.
- Current price and currency, if available and verified.
- Available product attributes, such as colour, material, size, or style, when supplied by the source.
- Product link and last-checked timestamp, when available.
- A clearly visible View Original Product action.

Opening the original-product action should open the genuine product page from the retailer or authorized source.

The in-app detail view must explain where the product was found, for example, “Found on Amazon” or “Source: Flipkart,” using the actual source associated with that product.

Do not imply that a retailer endorses The Wedding Circle. Do not fabricate a source, product price, availability, rating, or destination URL.

If a product becomes unavailable, show its status honestly and avoid presenting stale prices as current.

3.4 Create five specialized discovery bots

Create a bot-management system with five independently configurable discovery agents:

Bot 1 — Amazon India Bridal Trends

- Discover relevant Indian bridal clothing, jewellery, makeup, and accessories from Amazon India.

Bot 2 — Flipkart Bridal Trends

- Discover relevant Indian bridal clothing, jewellery, makeup, and accessories from Flipkart.

Bot 3 — Meesho Bridal Trends

- Discover relevant Indian bridal clothing, jewellery, makeup, and accessories from Meesho.

Bot 4 — Bridal Dress Research

- Focus on trending Indian bridal dresses, lehengas, sarees, reception outfits, wedding suits, and related clothing across approved sources.

Bot 5 — Bridal Jewellery Research

- Focus on bridal jewellery sets, necklaces, earrings, bangles, maang tikka, nath, kaleere, and related accessories across approved sources.

The dress and jewellery bots may use approved sources beyond the first three retailers, provided each product's actual source is recorded and displayed.

Bot responsibilities

Each bot should:

1. Search for products relevant to Indian bridal fashion and wedding accessories.
2. Identify products with evidence of popularity, growing interest, or emerging trends.
3. Rank candidates using available, legitimate signals such as retailer-provided popularity, bestseller status, verified review counts, recent engagement data where accessible, and trend information from permitted sources.
4. Prefer relevant, available, well-documented products over unrelated or low-quality matches.
5. Deduplicate repeated products across bots and categories.
6. Store the product metadata and original source link.
7. Record when the product was discovered and last checked.
8. Update or remove outdated results according to configurable freshness rules.
9. Never invent engagement numbers, rankings, ratings, sales, or claims that a product is viral.

Do not assume that every retailer provides public APIs or allows automated crawling. Inspect available authorized APIs, affiliate/product feeds, permitted search services, and their terms before choosing an integration.

Use official or authorized integrations where available. If a source requires API credentials, affiliate approval, or additional configuration, show that status clearly in Developer controls. Do not bypass anti-bot protections, access controls, or retailer restrictions.

If a bot cannot access a source yet, display it as Not configured or Needs connection rather than pretending that it is searching.

Bot execution and reliability

- Support configurable search schedules and manual runs.
- Avoid unnecessary repeated requests and duplicate product ingestion.
- Implement appropriate rate limits, timeouts, retries, and error handling.
- Keep bot jobs independent so a failure in one bot does not stop the others.
- Store search-run status, last successful run, number of valid products found, and meaningful errors.
- Use existing backend infrastructure where practical; do not run continuous polling inside a user's browser.
- Keep product discovery asynchronous so Discover remains responsive.
