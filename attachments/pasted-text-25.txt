1. Automatic 30-Day Wedding Expiry and Paid Media Retention

B. Send reminders during the final seven days

Starting exactly seven days before "expires_at", send two reminders per day to the wedding's authorized host and co-hosts.

Requirements:

- Send two reminders daily, approximately 12 hours apart, until the wedding expires or retention is extended.
- Use the existing notification infrastructure if it supports reliable scheduled delivery. Otherwise, implement a suitable server-side scheduled job or equivalent mechanism.
- Do not rely on the user's browser being open or their phone staying online.
- Prevent duplicate reminders through persistent scheduling records or idempotency keys.
- Respect notification preferences where applicable, but ensure the expiry deadline and in-app notice remain visible.
- Notify only authorized hosts and co-hosts, never all wedding guests.
- Include the wedding name and exact expiry date/time in each reminder.

Include two clear actions:

1. Save my media — guide the host/co-host through the existing media download or export capability.
2. Extend for ₹150 — open the extension/payment flow.

D. Expire and delete the wedding safely

At the exact expiry deadline, automatically remove the expired wedding and its associated data according to the app's retention rules.

This includes:

- Wedding record and associated private content.
- Photos and videos stored in cloud storage.
- Chat messages and associated attachments.
- Other wedding-specific content that should expire with the wedding.

However, delete nothing until you have verified that the wedding has not been extended and is genuinely eligible for expiry.

Implementation safeguards:

- Use an authoritative server-side scheduled cleanup process, not a client-side timer.
- Recheck "expires_at", the media-retention deadline, and any valid paid extension immediately before deletion.
- Handle retries and partial failures safely.
- Delete only resources belonging to the expired wedding. Never wipe shared storage, unrelated weddings, other users' data, or application-wide tables.
- Handle storage deletion and database cleanup consistently so a failed storage deletion is retried instead of leaving orphaned files.
- Prevent stale browser caches, offline queues, background sync, subscriptions, or pending requests from resurrecting a deleted wedding.
- Make expired weddings unavailable to guests as appropriate and display an expiry notice.
- Maintain only the minimal operational deletion/payment audit records required by the application's policy and applicable law; do not retain deleted media or chat content in the audit log.
- Ensure unpaid expired media is deleted according to the confirmed retention policy, while paid extensions are honored.

If wedding records, media, or chats are stored across multiple providers, identify the actual storage locations first and implement cleanup for each relevant system.
