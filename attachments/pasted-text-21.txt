3. Apply every theme throughout the wedding

Each theme must style the entire wedding experience consistently:

- Wedding landing/Home page and couple header.
- Ceremony cards and wedding information.
- Chat background, incoming/outgoing bubbles, bubble tails, and composer.
- Live page, camera preview, controls, comments, and supporting elements.
- Albums, image/video previews, and media controls.
- More section, buttons, forms, navigation surfaces, and dialogs.
- Relevant borders, shadows, text, icons, overlays, and selected states.

Do not allow components to retain unrelated hardcoded colours when a theme is selected. Preserve readable contrast and each theme's intended light/dark character.

4. Theme-specific animations

Implement restrained, lightweight animations appropriate to each theme:

- Butterfly Romance: slow butterfly drift.
- Moonlight Reverie: gentle star twinkle and moon glow.
- Monsoon Romance: subtle rain trails and soft reflections.
- Anime Ever After: occasional falling cherry-blossom petals.
- Royal Palace: restrained gold shimmer.
- Heaven's Embrace: slow cloud movement and soft light rays.
- Enchanted Garden: slowly drifting fireflies.
- Cosmic Destiny: gentle star movement and faint nebula transitions.
- Velvet Rose: occasional falling rose petals.
- Aurora Dreams: slowly flowing aurora gradients.

Animations must be decorative only and must not block interaction, cause layout shifts, or interfere with chat scrolling, camera controls, or video playback.

Use lightweight CSS or existing animation utilities. Avoid continuous expensive JavaScript animation loops, unnecessary dependencies, and excessive DOM elements. Respect reduced-motion preferences and disable or simplify decorative movement when requested by the operating system.
