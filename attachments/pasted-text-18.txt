- Avoid sharp triangular tips, abrupt corners, detached-looking tails, or awkward gaps.
- Align the tail correctly with the corresponding sender's avatar, accounting for different message lengths, text wrapping, and media messages.
- Keep the profile avatar the same height as the message bubble or slightly smaller. Never make the avatar larger than the bubble.
- Preserve the existing message alignment and layout.
- Retain the existing peach floral wedding theme, delicate gold accents, white/cream incoming bubbles, and solid golden bride/groom bubbles.
