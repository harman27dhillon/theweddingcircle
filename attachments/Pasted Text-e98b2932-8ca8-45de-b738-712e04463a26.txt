TASK 3 — Make the invitation adapt to the selected religion and tradition

The invitation must reflect the religion or wedding tradition selected during creation of that specific wedding.

Do not apply one generic Indian wedding design to every wedding.

A. Use the actual saved selection

- Read the religion/tradition and theme saved for the wedding.
- Use them to determine suitable visual motifs, colors, decorative borders, invitation wording and optional traditional elements.
- Keep the host's chosen theme customizable independently of religion.
- If religion/tradition is unspecified, use a neutral, elegant invitation design.
- Do not infer religion from names, photographs, location or other indirect clues.

B. Respect different traditions

Use these as design directions, not rigid templates:

Sikh weddings

- Consider refined Punjabi invitation styling, tasteful floral or phulkari-inspired patterns and appropriate Punjabi design elements.
- Use terms such as Anand Karaj only when relevant to the actual wedding schedule.
- Include religious symbols, prayers or blessings only when the host has chosen or approved them.

Hindu weddings

- Consider suitable Indian ornamental motifs, floral decoration and auspicious design elements appropriate to the selected wedding style.
- Reflect the actual ceremonies configured by the host rather than assuming every ceremony is included.

Muslim weddings

- Consider elegant geometric patterns and tasteful arabesque-inspired decoration.
- Use culturally appropriate invitation wording only where appropriate and approved.

Christian weddings

- Consider refined floral styling, elegant typography and understated religious motifs where desired.

Other traditions or secular weddings

- Use a respectful design suited to the selected tradition or a neutral premium invitation when appropriate.

Avoid stereotypes and respect regional, family and cultural differences. Do not automatically insert religious symbols, prayers, scripture, blessings or ceremonial text without the host's approval.

Different weddings should be able to display genuinely different invitation designs based on their saved settings.
