Redesign ONLY the main/landing page of The Wedding Circle.

Do NOT change the app's underlying functionality, routing, database, authentication, wedding-circle logic, or other pages.

The goal is to make the landing page feel like a premium modern Indian wedding product, not a developer prototype.

1. Hero section

Make the first screen visually strong and immediately explain the product.

Headline:
Your Wedding. Everyone Together.

Subheadline:
One private space for your family, friends and wedding moments — before, during and after the big day.

Primary CTA:
Create Wedding Circle — ₹499

Secondary CTA:
Explore as Guest

Add a beautiful, subtle Indian wedding visual/background treatment. Keep it elegant and modern — not overly flashy, not stereotypical Bollywood.

2. Show the product visually

Instead of explaining everything with large blocks of text, show 3 attractive visual cards:

LIVE
Share wedding moments and updates in real time.

FAMILY
Keep both families connected in one private space.

MEMORIES
Photos, videos and moments stay together after the wedding.

These should look like actual product features, not generic marketing cards.

3. Explain the concept simply

Add a short section:

One wedding. One private circle.

Explain in 2–3 short sentences that the couple/families create a private wedding space where invited people can follow updates, participate, share memories and stay connected even if they cannot physically attend.

Do NOT use long paragraphs.

4. Pricing

Keep ₹499 visible, but don't make pricing the first thing users see.

Use a clean card:

Create your Wedding Circle
₹499
One wedding space for the entire celebration.

Button:
Create Wedding Circle

If the existing product already supports bride-side/groom-side collaboration, mention that clearly but briefly.

5. Visual design

Use:

- premium ivory/cream base
- subtle wedding-inspired accent colors
- elegant typography
- generous spacing
- rounded cards
- subtle shadows
- tasteful animations
- polished mobile-first layout

Avoid:

- excessive gradients
- excessive text
- huge empty areas
- generic SaaS/dashboard appearance
- too many buttons
- excessive animations
- cheap-looking wedding clipart

6. Navigation

Keep navigation extremely simple.

Logo:
The Wedding Circle

Navigation:
How It Works
Explore as Guest
Create Wedding

On mobile, use a clean compact menu.

7. Social proof / emotional section

Add a small section near the bottom:

Because not everyone can be there.

Explain that The Wedding Circle lets people who cannot attend physically still feel part of the wedding through live updates, photos, videos and family moments.

This is one of the strongest emotional reasons to use the product, so make it visually meaningful.

8. Final CTA

End with:

Make everyone part of your wedding.

Button:
Create Your Wedding Circle

Important

Do not rewrite the entire application.

Modify ONLY the landing/main page.

Preserve all existing functionality and links.

Make the result feel like a real consumer product that could be launched publicly, with the visual quality of a modern premium wedding/lifestyle app.

Before finishing, check the page carefully on both desktop and mobile widths and fix spacing, overflow, button sizing and typography issues.
