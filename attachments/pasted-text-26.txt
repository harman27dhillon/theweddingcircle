- Conditional rendering that hides the navigation on particular routes or tabs.
- Incorrect positioning, height calculations, or overflow rules.

- Nested scrolling containers or page wrappers that push the navigation off-screen.
- Theme-specific CSS that changes visibility, stacking order, positioning, or background colors.

- Keep the bottom navigation visible and fixed to the bottom of the wedding app viewport across Home, Chat, Live, Album, and More.
