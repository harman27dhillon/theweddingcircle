- Background gradients, textures, artwork, and lighting.
- Decorative floral, botanical, celestial, architectural, or other theme-specific assets.
- Individual border and corner treatments.
- Couple-name typography, alignment, spacing, and ampersand placement.
- A designed divider with the correct ornament.
- Chat and Live shortcut buttons with the intended shape, layered borders, shadows, and accents.
- A well-positioned date and location row.
- Appropriate responsive layout and decorative depth.

Use real assets already available in the project or add suitable, legally usable artwork. Do not invent asset paths or leave missing asset references. If a particular asset is unavailable, create an appropriate CSS/SVG treatment or report the missing asset.
