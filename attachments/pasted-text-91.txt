## 2. Restore the exact SEEK game

SEEK is a smart game-host bot inside Wedding Circle Chat.

**Trigger command:**
`@seek start who said it`

Expected game flow:

1. A participant types the trigger command in Chat.
2. SEEK starts the game and displays a compact temporary game bar/message inside Chat.
3. Participants submit their statements during a live 1-minute submission timer.
4. SEEK displays the submitted statements without revealing who wrote each one.
5. The person who started the game gets 2 minutes to match each statement with the correct live participant.
6. The starter locks their answers.
7. SEEK reveals the results:
   - Correct matches: green.
   - Incorrect matches: red.
8. Display the final results clearly inside Chat.

## 3. Essential behaviour

- Only eligible participants currently in the wedding should participate.
- Each statement must remain anonymous until the reveal.
- The submission timer must be real and synchronized for all participants.
- The 2-minute guessing timer must also be synchronized.
- Prevent duplicate submissions and unauthorized answer changes.
- Handle participants leaving or disconnecting during a game.
- Prevent multiple simultaneous games from accidentally overwriting one another.
- Support multiple users participating through the existing real-time architecture.
- Game state must remain consistent across devices and reconnects.
- Show clear messages when the game is unavailable, expired, or completed.
